The problem

How does the Calvin cycle fix carbon?

In the stroma of the chloroplast, the enzyme RuBisCO attaches CO₂ to a five-carbon sugar, RuBP 1. The unstable six-carbon product splits at once into two molecules of 3-PGA, which are reduced to G3P using ATP and NADPH from the light reactions 2. For every three CO₂ fixed, one G3P leaves the cycle to build sugars; the rest regenerates RuBP so the cycle can turn again 13.

Privacy

Private by design. Yours by default.

Bloom sees a lot, so it's built to earn that trust. Here's the deal, in plain words.

Local-first

Your library lives on your own machine, not in someone else's cloud.

You approve every capture

Screen captures wait in an inbox until you keep or discard them.

Sensitive apps stay private

Password managers, messages and banking apps are blocked by default. Add your own.

Your choice of AI

Run local models (Ollama), or connect your own API key. You decide what leaves your device.

Pause anytime

One click stops all capture. Resume whenever you're ready.

Leave with everything

Export your wiki to Obsidian and your cards to Anki, whenever you like.

The science

Built on how learning actually works.

Bloom's design follows the research — including the parts that say what not to do.

Seeing progress helps you steer.

Across 138 randomized trials, monitoring progress toward a goal helped people reach it — more so when progress was recorded and reported.

Harkin et al., 2016 · Psychological Bulletin

So Bloom keeps an honest record and shows it to you weekly.

Grades can backfire.

In a landmark review, over a third of feedback interventions made performance worse.

Kluger & DeNisi, 1996 · Psychological Bulletin

So the Mirror describes and cites. It never scores you.

Mixing beats blocking.

Interleaving topics during practice improves long-term retention compared with drilling one topic at a time.

Rohrer & Pashler, 2010 · Educational Researcher

So your reviews shuffle concepts together.

A little difficulty helps it stick.

Learning that feels harder in the moment — spacing, retrieval, variety — tends to last longer.

Bjork, 1994 · “desirable difficulties”

So Bloom schedules reviews for the edge of forgetting.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

When can I use Bloom?
We're in private development and raising our first round. Early access will open in waves, and the waitlist gets invites first.
What will it cost?
We'll share pricing before launch. People on the waitlist hear first.
Do I have to take notes?
No. Bloom writes the wiki from what you gather. You can edit any page, but you never start from a blank one.
What exactly does Bloom capture?
Only the sources you connect. On a Mac, optional Visual Memory reads the text on your screen — and audio, if you allow it — and every item waits in your inbox until you keep it. Sensitive apps are excluded by default.
Where does my data live?
On your own machine. Bloom is local-first: your library, wiki and reviews are stored locally, and you choose whether AI runs locally or through your own API key.
Which devices will it support?
Bloom runs in your browser. Visual Memory capture is macOS-only for now.
Is this another chatbot?
No — it's closer to a memory. Chatbots answer from the internet; Bloom answers from what you've read, and cites it. You can even import your ChatGPT and Claude history.
Are you raising?
Yes. If you back learning or privacy-first software, we'd love to talk: hello@example.com
